Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

synch-for-life is a private, personal-use application operated by Adam Trina. It is not offered to the public, and its only user is Adam Trina.

What data it accesses

With Adam's own permission, synch-for-life reads his own Google Calendar (read-only) to show his schedule in his private dashboard. It does not access anyone else's Google account.

How data is used and stored

Data is stored only on Adam Trina's own computers and is used only to provide his personal dashboard and reminders. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API data

synch-for-life's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
